- Champion entry vs mid (adverse, n=2000 sample): GBPUSD mean **0.69 pip**; better-than-mid 2.2%. Expected slip 0.2 pip + half-spread.
- ATR14: GBPUSD mean **18.0 pips** => 2*ATR TP ~36.0 pips. Matches avg win size below.
- net_pnl is fractional return (exit-entry)/entry (signed), not dollar pip-value. Same formula all pairs.
- total_return = compound prod(1+r)-1. GBPUSD: sum r=6.428 => compound 604.22 exactly. Same compounding story as USDJPY (high expectancy + n) vs EURUSD (lower expectancy => 11.17). Not a unit bug.
