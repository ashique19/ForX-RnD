HYPOTHESIS 2026-10-02: TIME-STOP DD CONTROL (HORIZON 6)

Mechanism
---------
Reduce the maximum holding horizon from 8 to 6 one-hour bars. This is a
trade-lifetime cap, not a tighter stop: TP/SL distances, entry filters,
cost model, sizing, sessions, weekday gate, and confidence remain unchanged.
A shorter maximum exposure should cut prolonged adverse excursions and cap
loss clustering from stale positions, explicitly targeting lower max DD.

Knob and scope
--------------
The existing `horizon` knob in config/default.yaml is changed 8 -> 6. The
same setting is used causally for triple-barrier labels and Replay timeout;
no new feature or look-ahead is introduced. This is a single EURUSD 1h,
2015-01-01-to-history-end, costs-on full-history Replay using bid/ask data and
0.2 pip adverse slippage per fill with exit slippage enabled.

Frozen / not touched
--------------------
No promote, live-gate, sessions, min_confidence, weekday_gate, portfolio_brain,
measured-intel, or failed-family retuning. In particular, news-blackout-
expanded, tight-stop 1.75, min_tp_pips=16, min_vol_regime=0.8,
size-by-confidence, purge+embargo, vol_scaled_conf, and ADX/trend 3/3 remain
frozen/off as currently configured.

Decision rule
-------------
Compare the after-cost full-history Replay champion book against its challenger.
A pass requires challenger PF > current champion PF, challenger max DD not
worse (must be >= champion DD), and total return > champion return, with the
existing retrain/promotion result remaining null because promotion is disabled
for this research run. Any fail, error, or non-beat reverts default.yaml to
config/default.yaml.bak_pre_horizon6_20261002 and records a strike.
