## One-line hypothesis
Skip only entries whose causal 2.0-ATR target distance is below 16 pips (`signals.min_tp_pips=16.0`), because tiny-target / low-excursion trades expose the book to a larger fixed spread-and-slippage fraction and adverse chop; unlike a tighter stop, this leaves SL/TP geometry unchanged for trades that remain.

## Why this is distinct
- This is a target-distance eligibility floor, not stop ATR, news blackout, confidence scaling, position sizing, purge/embargo, ADX/trend regime, session, or portfolio-brain logic.
- The prior screen used `min_tp_pips=8`, which was effectively a no-op on the cached sample (about 99.99% of causal 2-ATR targets were already >=8 pips). This pre-registers 16 pips, near the lower-tail boundary, so it is a distinct and measurable filter rather than a retune of a failed family.
- Cached EURUSD H1 causal target-distance distribution: p01 11.6 pips, p05 14.6, p10 16.5, median 26.3; 16 pips would screen roughly 9% of bars before model-side eligibility, without changing fills or exits.
