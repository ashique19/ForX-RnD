**Reading.**
- Swaps are strongly asymmetric. The broker keeps roughly 2 %/yr between the long and short sides relative to the BIS policy differential.
  For example, EURUSD: diff -1.375 pp, long -3.51, short +1.49. USDCHF: diff +3.875, long +3.60, short -5.67.
- The old 1 pip/night markup was too harsh for USD-base longs and too mild for JPY/CHF shorts.
  At the 2-ATR scale a week of holding costs up to about 0.09R (EURGBP long, USDCHF short) or earns up to about 0.06R.
- **Limitation:** these are today's swaps. Applied to 2015-2025 history, when policy rates were very different, they are only a stress test, not a historical record.
