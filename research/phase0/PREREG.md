## What Phase 0 measures

Diagnostic only. No pass, no promote, no yaml edit, no change to `min_confidence` (0.60), the 2 ATR / 2 ATR barriers, the 8-bar hold, session settings, or risk.

1. Whether training labels in `walk_forward_backtest` overlap each test window, given an 8-bar forward label, and whether `holdout.train_mask` purges anything other than the final cutoff.
2. If they overlap: profit factor and expectancy of the production (unpurged) walk-forward against a purged and embargoed refit that calls the existing `fit_predict_bundle`.
3. Walk-forward out-of-sample predictions, saved if present, otherwise generated by `walk_forward_backtest`. Zero-cost and after-cost expectancy versus random sides (same decision times, many seeds) and versus flipped sides, with a time-block bootstrap.
4. Confidence deciles against realized net R, and P&L by exit reason (stop, target, time).
5. Bars where the stop and the target are both touched, and the share of trades.
6. Research round-trip cost (EURUSD 1.4 pips, USDJPY 2.4 pips) against the typical absolute 8-bar move, by pair and session. Reported only. No filter is changed.

Pairs: EURUSD and USDJPY.
