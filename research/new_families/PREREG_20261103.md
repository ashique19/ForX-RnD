# Pre-registration: multi-timeframe (4h + daily) confluence filter on the main-book 1h entries - 2026-11-03

Written and committed BEFORE any filtered result is computed. Research only (owner request t545u, 2026-11-03 11:35 Dhaka: "try graphs of different timeframes to identify more confident shots").

## Hypotheses
- **H1 (the idea).** The 1h model's entries are better when the higher timeframes (4h and daily) agree with their direction, so a confluence filter lifts after-cost PF above the unfiltered baseline and above 1.0.
- **H0.** The filter only removes trades. PF stays at or below the baseline and the baseline stays below 1.0 after measured costs.
- Prior evidence against H1 (declared, so the bar is not lowered):
  - 2026-10-20 (`research/htf/RESULTS_20261020.md`): the baseline loses on all 10 pairs over 10 years (in-sample PF 0.760, EURUSD 0.95; holdout 0.724). A daily SMA20/50 filter (F1) and a daily close-vs-SMA20 filter (F2) did not clear the bar in-sample (PF 0.753 / 0.765) or in the holdout (0.687 / 0.766).
  - `reports/experiments.md`: a 4h MTF gate plus min-conf on the 2-year desk data lowered PF (0.85 / 0.79).
  - Overlap: variant (b) is a close cousin of the banned F1/F2 daily filters and is run only as a counted re-test with a different definition (EMA50). Failed variants get banned.

## Entries, data and simulation (identical to the 2026-10-20 baseline)
- Entries: the saved main-book walk-forward predictions `data/edge_check/htf_20261020/<PAIR>_preds.csv` (champion recipe via `walk_forward_backtest` on validated `data/history/<PAIR>_1h.csv`: xgboost, train 2000 / test 250 / step 250, min_conf 0.60, TP 2 ATR / SL 2 ATR, horizon 8 bars, next-open entry, one position; holdout cutoff 2025-10-01 enforced, no fold trains at or after it). No retrain, no model or yaml change. Columns used: `pred` (post-filter signal), `confidence` (P of the predicted class).
- The baseline is `pred` unchanged. A variant sets `pred` to HOLD where its condition fails, then re-simulates with the same simulator (one position, next-open entry, TP/SL 2/2 ATR, 8 bars).
- Pairs: EURUSD and USDJPY are each judged on their own. The other 8 pairs, pooled with them (10-pair run), are a backup label only (needs pooled PF above the pooled baseline by 0.05 and at least 6 of 10 pairs above their baseline by 0.02 with at least 30 trades).
- In-sample = entries before 2025-10-01 (first entry 2015-05). Holdout = entries from 2025-10-01.

## Higher-timeframe bars (built only by resampling the validated 1h history; nothing is synthesized)
- **4h bars:** bins start at 01/05/09/13/17/21 UTC, bar end = start + 4h. Open = first, High = max, Low = min, Close = last available 1h bar. Bins with fewer than 2 hourly bars are dropped.
- **Daily bars:** New York 17:00 days exactly as `daily_bars()` in `research/htf/daily_trend_flip_wf_20261020.py` (day ends 17:00 America/New_York; days with fewer than 12 hourly bars dropped; close = last hourly close).
- **No lookahead (critical).** A 1h signal on bar t is decided when bar t closes, at D = t + 1h, and enters at the next Open. A higher-timeframe bar may be used only if its end is at or before D. A bar that is still forming is never used and never partially used. The trend value used at D is the value computed from completed bars only. Explicit tests in `tests/test_confluence_20261103.py` check this on synthetic and real data: values from full data equal values from data truncated at the decision bar, a violent move inside the forming 4h bar changes nothing, the used bar's end is at or before D, and a swing pivot is unusable until its confirming bars are complete.

## Trend definitions (fixed now)
- **T4 (4h trend):** sign(Close4h - EMA50 of the 4h closes), `ewm(span=50, adjust=False, min_periods=50)` over completed 4h bars. Undefined or exactly 0 means no trend.
- **TD (daily trend):** sign(CloseD - EMA50 of the daily closes), same EMA settings over completed daily bars.
- **TS (4h structure):** swing pivots on completed 4h bars with k = 2: bar i is a swing high if its High is strictly above the Highs of the 2 bars before and the 2 bars after; swing low likewise on Lows. A pivot is confirmed only when bar i+2 has completed. Uptrend (+1) = the last two confirmed swing highs rise AND the last two confirmed swing lows rise (HH and HL). Downtrend (-1) = both fall (LH and LL). Anything else (or fewer than two of each) = 0.
- A signal "agrees" when BUY meets +1 or SELL meets -1. A trend of 0 or undefined never agrees (the entry is skipped).

## Variants (7 trials: six candidates (a)-(f) plus one control (g); N goes 167 -> 174, alpha = 0.05/174 = 2.87e-4)
- (a) **H4_EMA50:** take the entry only if T4 agrees.
- (b) **D_EMA50:** only if TD agrees.
- (c) **H4_AND_D:** only if T4 and TD both agree.
- (d) **H4_D_CONF65:** (c) and 1h confidence at least 0.65.
- (e) **H4_STRUCT:** only if TS agrees.
- (f) **H4_D_DISAGREE (counter-check):** only if T4 and TD are both opposite to the signal. Expected worse than the baseline. It is a sanity test and is not judged for a pass.
- (g) **CONF65 (control, no higher timeframe):** confidence at least 0.65 only. It is the comparator for (d) so that a gain from "more confidence" is not credited to confluence.
- Diagnostic, not a trial: the baseline trades are split into three buckets by T4 and TD agreement (both agree / mixed / both oppose) and PF is reported per bucket. If the filter carries information, PF should rise from "oppose" to "agree".

## Costs and financing
- Judged: round-trip `mt5_measured` (forex_lab.cost_model; EURUSD 1.4, USDJPY 2.4 pips; equals the p90 round trip of the earlier rounds), plus the measured MT5 swap snapshot (`data/research/swaps/mt5_swaps_20261021_1813Z.json`, interest-on-price per year / 360, Wednesday x3, signed, rollover = 17:00 New York). A trade is charged for each rollover between its entry bar open and the end of its exit bar (conservative, slightly over-counts).
- Reference, reported but not judged: 1.0 pip round trip with no swap; mt5_measured without swap.
- The swap snapshot is a single 2026-10 reading applied to history. With holds of at most 8 hours its effect is small.

## Metrics
Per trade R = net_return x entry / (2 x ATR14 at the decision bar). PF = sum of positive R / |sum of negative R|. Max drawdown in cumulative R by entry time. One-sided t-test of mean R > 0. Folds = 5 calendar blocks over [2015-01-01, 2025-10-01); a fold is positive if its net R > 0.

## Pass criteria (fixed before running; each of EURUSD and USDJPY is a separate case; variants (a)-(e) and (g) are judged)
**In-sample, all must hold at the judged cost:**
1. Trades at least 100. Fewer than 100 is UNDERPOWERED and cannot pass. 100 to 299 is flagged "thin".
2. PF at least 1.05 AND at least the same pair's baseline PF + 0.05.
3. Net R above 0 and |max DD| not larger than net R.
4. One-sided p for mean R above 0 below 2.87e-4.
5. At least 3 of 5 folds positive.
6. For (d) only: PF at least PF of (g) + 0.03.

**Holdout, scored once, only for cases that pass in-sample:** at least 30 trades, PF above 1.0 and at least the baseline holdout PF + 0.05, net R above 0, one-sided p below 0.05 / (number of cases scored).
**Overall PASS = in-sample AND holdout.** There is no re-selection, threshold change or definition change after any result is seen. The trade-count change versus the baseline is reported for every variant. Any pass only leads to a written proposal for a frozen forward paper shadow; nothing is enabled live.
