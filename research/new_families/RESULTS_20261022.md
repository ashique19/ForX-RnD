## Verdict
**0/15 variants pass. All 15 are now banned** (no re-runs under new names).
- A (session-open fade of a large Asia/day move): loses everywhere, with pooled PF 0.65-0.69 and 0/10 pairs above 1. It agrees with the 2026-10-14 session mean-reversion failure.
- B (squeeze breakout): the closest to break-even. USDJPY N24/q20 has PF 1.026 at p90 and 1.22 at p50, but with p = 0.38 it is far from significant and below the 1.10 bar.
  Elsewhere it loses (pooled PF 0.67-0.71, 1/10 pairs above 1). The edge is about cost-sized, and a squeeze makes the ATR stop small, so costs weigh more in R.
- C (PIT event reaction, cont and fade): the worst family, at pooled PF 0.50-0.56. Both directions lose, so the post-release bar has no exploitable drift at 1h resolution, and costs plus a whipsawed 1-ATR barrier dominate.
- D (cooldown after a stop-out): it improves the champion, but not to an edge. EURUSD PF goes 0.948 -> 0.975 (K=8), and pooled goes 0.760 -> 0.779 (K=48), with DD reduced.
  It is a damage limiter on a negative-edge model, not an edge.
