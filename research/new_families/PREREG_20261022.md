# Pre-registration: new entry families A-D - 2026-10-22 (written and committed before any run)

**Scope.** Research only. No yaml edits, no promote, no live-gate change, no /pipeline/*, no /model/train/*, no writes to models/, journals, MT5 or the desk.
Script: `research/new_families/new_families_20261022.py`. Outputs go to the ignored `data/edge_check/new_families_20261022/`.
Each family runs as its own background process on the PC at Idle/BelowNormal priority. Nothing is retrained.

**Why.** The logistic/xgboost champion entries have no after-cost edge (entry-edge round 0/3, N=131).
This round asks whether any structurally different entry signal has a positive edge after costs.

## Focus (declared now, not changed after results)
- **Primary evaluation:** EURUSD and USDJPY, **each its own pass test** (liquid, lowest measured costs).
- **Secondary / robustness:** all 10 desk pairs (EURUSD, USDJPY, GBPUSD, AUDUSD, NZDUSD, USDCAD, USDCHF, EURJPY, EURGBP, GBPJPY).
  These are reported per pair and pooled, and give a robustness label only (below).

## Common harness (same as the entry-edge round)
- **Data:** validated `data/history/<PAIR>_1h.csv`, UTC, indexed by bar open time, from 2015-01 to the last bar (2026-10-12). Used as is, with no fill and no synthetic bars.
- **Closed bars only.** A signal is computed at the close of decision bar t using bars <= t. Entry is at the **Open of bar t+1**.
  No lookahead is unit-tested: a signal at t computed on data truncated at t must equal the full-data signal.
- **Exit:** `forex_lab.backtest._first_touch_exit` (High/Low path, SL wins ties, timeout at the horizon close), with SL/TP = multiples of ATR14 (true range) at the decision bar.
  One position per pair, and no new entry until the bar after the exit.
- **Costs:** primary = round trip 2 x measured one-way **p90** (`data/paper_shadow/measured_costs.csv`), constant per pair: EURUSD 1.4 pips, USDJPY 2.4 pips.
  Secondary = 2 x p50 (reported, not judged).
- **R per trade** = net_return x entry / (SL_mult x ATR14 at the decision bar).
- **Split:** holdout cutoff 2025-10-01 00:00 UTC (`research/holdout.json`). In-sample = entries before the cutoff; holdout = entries at or after it.
  The in-sample phase passes the simulator only bars < cutoff + 3 days (exits of late in-sample trades) and keeps only entries < cutoff.
  The holdout is run **only** for (variant, primary pair) cases that pass in-sample, after the in-sample table is written and committed.
- **Metrics:** PF = gross +R / gross -R, net R, max DD in R (cumulative R by entry time), trades, win rate, and a one-sided t-test on per-trade R.

## Families and fixed grids (15 variants; no other values will be tried)
**A - session-open fade of an over-extended Asia move (4 variants).** Weekdays only. The day anchor is the Open of that UTC day's 00:00 bar (the case is skipped if that bar is missing).
- `LDN`: decision bar = the 06:00 bar (closes 07:00). The move is m = Close(06:00 bar) - Open(00:00 bar). Entry at the 07:00 Open.
- `NY`: decision bar = the 12:00 bar. m = Close(12:00 bar) - Open(00:00 bar). Entry at the 13:00 Open.
- Trigger: |m| >= k x ATR14(decision bar), with k in {2.0, 3.0}. Side = -sign(m), i.e. fade the move.
- Exit: SL 1.5 ATR, TP 1.5 ATR, horizon 6 bars.
- Variants: A_LDN_k2, A_LDN_k3, A_NY_k2, A_NY_k3.
- Relation to failed rule 8 of 2026-10-14 ("session mean reversion": fade the first close beyond the Asia high/low at any hour 07-16, exit 16:00):
  - That rule had no extension size, no ATR barrier and no fixed open time. A tests only large moves measured from the day open, entered at the open hour with ATR exits.
  - It is still the closest relative, and rule 8's loss is prior evidence against A.

**B - volatility-compression breakout (4 variants).**
- Squeeze: the percentile rank of ATR14 at t-1 within ATR14 over bars t-500..t-1 is <= q, with q in {0.10, 0.20}.
- Range: max High / min Low over bars t-N..t-1, with N in {12, 24}. BUY if Close_t > range high; SELL if Close_t < range low.
- Only decision bars with open hour 07:00-15:00 UTC (London + NY), weekdays.
- Exit: SL 1.0 ATR, TP 2.0 ATR, horizon 12 bars.
- Variants: B_N12_q10, B_N12_q20, B_N24_q10, B_N24_q20.
- Relation to failed rules: rule 7 (Asia-range breakout, no squeeze, exit 16:00) and Donchian 20/55 (daily, no squeeze). The squeeze precondition and the ATR barrier are new; those failures are prior evidence against B.

**C - post high-impact event reaction (4 variants).**
- Events: the real point-in-time calendar `data/calendars/pit_high_impact_usd_eur_rich_cpi_pce_gdp.json` (n=933, built 2026-09-25 from BLS/ALFRED/BEA/Fed/ECB release dates: NFP, Unemployment Rate, CPI, Core PCE, GDP, FOMC, ECB Main Refinancing Rate).
  No events are synthesized. Events sharing a timestamp are merged. Only events inside the history range are used.
- Event bar = the bar whose open is floor(event time, 1h); it must exist. Decision at its close. Reaction = Close - Open of the event bar.
- Trigger: |reaction| >= k x ATR14 at the bar **before** the event bar, with k in {0.5, 1.0}. Mode cont = trade in the reaction direction; fade = against it.
- Exit: SL 1.0 ATR(pre-event), TP 1.0 ATR(pre-event), horizon 6 bars. R uses the same ATR.
- A pair trades an event only if the event currency is in the pair: USD events -> the 7 USD pairs; EUR events -> EURUSD, EURJPY, EURGBP. GBPJPY gets none.
- Variants: C_cont_k05, C_cont_k10, C_fade_k05, C_fade_k10.
- Calendar limitations, as recorded by its builder: no consensus/surprise (by design: no free PIT source without lookahead); EUR HICP/GDP are absent.
- Related: news-blackout gates were tested before as filters on the champion, never as an entry family.

**D - re-entry cooldown overlay on the existing model (3 variants).**
- The baseline is the saved walk-forward champion predictions (`data/edge_check/htf_20261020/<PAIR>_preds.csv`), simulated exactly as the entry-edge baseline.
  The baseline must reproduce the HTF baseline trade count and total R, or the run stops.
- Overlay: after a trade exits with reason sl or sl_conflict at exit bar x, no new entry before bar x + 1 + K, with K in {8, 24, 48}. Everything else is unchanged.
- Variants: D_K8, D_K24, D_K48.
- Motivation: the main book re-bought EURGBP 4 times while price fell. This is research only and never wired live.

**Trials:** 15 new variants -> **N = 131 + 15 = 146**.

## Pass rules (p90 costs). The unit is (variant, primary pair).
**In-sample PASS (needed before any holdout look) = all of:**
1. PF >= 1.10.
2. Trades >= 150 (C: >= 100).
3. Controlled DD: |max DD R| <= net R (recovery factor >= 1).
4. One-sided t-test mean per-trade R > 0, p < 0.05 / 146 = **3.42e-4**.

**Holdout PASS (scored once, only for in-sample passes) = all of:**
1. PF > 1.10.
2. Net R > 0.
3. |max DD R| <= |in-sample max DD R|.
4. Trades >= 30 (C: >= 20).
5. One-sided t-test mean daily summed R > 0, p < 0.05 / (number of cases sent to holdout).

**Overall PASS** = in-sample PASS AND holdout PASS on EURUSD or on USDJPY (each case judged on its own).

**Robustness label** (reported, not required for PASS): all 10 pairs pooled, with the same in-sample criteria 1-3 (trades >= 500), and >= 6/10 pairs (C: majority of eligible pairs) with PF > 1.0.
If a variant passes on a primary pair, the 10-pair pooled holdout PF (> 1.0 needed for "robust") is also reported.

A PASS produces only a written proposal to Ashiqul (for example a paper shadow). It never creates a rule, a yaml change or a live gate.
Failed variants are banned and will not be re-run under new names.
