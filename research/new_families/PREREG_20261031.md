## Data, bars, costs, financing (all fixed now)
- **Data:** `data/history/<PAIR>_1h.csv` (validated, UTC, indexed by bar open), 2015-01-01 -> 2026-10-12 18:00 (EURJPY ends 2026-10-09 20:00). Used as is: no fill, no synthetic bars. A pair that lacks history is reported, not filled.
- **Bars:** exactly the 2026-10-22 low-frequency convention (`research/lowfreq/lowfreq_20261022.py`): trading day 21:00 -> 21:00 UTC, 4h bins starting 21/01/05/09/13/17 UTC, closed bars only, bar stamped at its close, **decision at a bar close, entry at the next bar's Open**.
- **Judged price cost:** `mt5_measured` round trip (pips, constant per pair): EURUSD 1.4, USDJPY 2.4, GBPUSD 2.8, AUDUSD 2.6, NZDUSD 3.2, USDCAD 3.6, USDCHF 3.0, EURJPY 4.0, EURGBP 2.8, GBPJPY 7.6 (from the committed table; the harness reads it through `forex_lab.cost_model` with `--cost-model mt5_measured`).
  Secondary, reported but not judged: `mt5_p90` (EURUSD 1.0, USDJPY 1.6) and `mt5_p50`.
- **Judged financing:** the measured OANDA demo swaps (`data/research/swaps/mt5_swaps_20261021_1813Z.json`, SWAPS_20261022.md). Per weekday 21:00 UTC rollover held, return += swap% / 100 / 360 of the held side; the Wednesday rollover counts x3 (7 nights/week).
  Limitation, as before: today's swaps are applied to all of history, so this tests survival under today's financing, not the historical P&L. Secondary (reported, not judged): BIS policy-rate accrual minus 1 pip per rollover (the 2026-10-22 primary).
- **Risk unit / R:** F1: SL distance (1.0 x ATR14 at the decision bar). All others: 2 x ATR14 (daily, true range) at the decision bar. R = net return x entry / risk unit, after cost and financing.
- **Split:** holdout cutoff 2025-10-01 00:00 UTC (`research/holdout.json`). In-sample = entries before the cutoff.
  Re-test variants (F1_*, F2_top3, F3_L120) use the 2026-10-22 convention: in-sample sees hourly data < cutoff + 30 days, positions open at its end close at the last close.
  The new LT variants see hourly data < cutoff only, and a position open at the cutoff is closed at the last in-sample close (flagged `data_end`), so no holdout-window price enters them.
  The holdout runs on all data, keeps entries >= cutoff, and runs **only** for (variant, pair) cases that pass in-sample, after the in-sample table is committed.
- **Walk-forward folds (nothing is fitted, so these are time-stability folds):** 5 equal-length calendar blocks between 2015-01-01 and 2025-10-01 (`pd.date_range(..., periods=6)`), assigned by entry time. A fold is positive if its net R > 0 (a fold with no trades is not positive).

## Variants (11 trials; no other values will be tried)
**Declared primary timeframe: daily is first-class.** F1 runs on both 4h and daily; every other family is daily.
1. **F1 squeeze breakout** - rules exactly as `PREREG_LOWFREQ_20261022.md`: percentile rank of ATR14(t-1) within the previous 250 bars <= q (0.10 or 0.20), Close_t beyond the previous 20-bar high/low, SL 1.0 ATR / TP 2.0 ATR, horizon 10 bars, one position per pair.
   Variants: `F1_4h_q10`, `F1_4h_q20`, `F1_D_q10`, `F1_D_q20`.
2. **F1 daily with a weekly-trend filter** (new): `F1_D_q10_W`, `F1_D_q20_W`. The F1_D entry is taken only if its side equals the weekly trend sign at the decision bar.
   - Weekly bars: the daily bars of one trading week (Mon-Fri trading-day labels; the Friday stub hour is in the Friday bar). The weekly close is the last daily close of the week, and the weekly bar exists only once that daily bar has closed.
   - Weekly trend = sign(WeeklyClose_k - SMA10 of the last 10 weekly closes ending at k). **k = the latest weekly bar completed strictly before the decision bar's close.** The week in progress is never used, and on a Friday the filter uses the previous week. No SMA value uses a bar after k.
   - A decision with no weekly trend yet (fewer than 10 completed weeks) is skipped. No standalone weekly-bar strategy is tested (too few trades).
3. **F2_top3 carry** - exactly as the low-frequency prereg (Friday daily close, BIS policy-rate differential, |d| >= 0.25, top 3 by |d|, 2 x ATR14 risk unit). Primary test = pooled book. **Declared: it had 20 in-sample trades under these signals, below its minimum of 30, so it cannot pass.** It is run for information; the minimum is not lowered.
4. **F3_L120 momentum** - the frozen MOM120 rule only: target = sign(Close_D / Close_{D-120} - 1), always in the market, reverse at the next Open. Confirmatory only: **no parameter, signal or exit change.** Primary cases EURUSD and USDJPY.
5. **Long-term trend family (new, daily, multi-week to multi-month holds):**
   - `LT1_SMA200`: target = +1 if Close > SMA200(Close), -1 if Close < SMA200, unchanged on equality. Entry and exit at the next Open when the close crosses (stop-and-reverse). 200 closed daily bars needed.
   - `LT2_DON55_20`: flat -> long when Close > the highest High of the previous 55 daily bars, short when Close < the lowest Low of the previous 55. A long exits when Close < the lowest Low of the previous 20 bars (a short: Close > the highest High of the previous 20). Exit and entry at the next Open. If an exit bar also triggers the opposite 55-day entry, the position reverses. Re-entry in the same direction is only allowed from a later decision bar.
   - `LT3_MA50_200`: target = sign(SMA50 - SMA200) of daily closes; reverse at the next Open on a cross.
   - All three: risk unit 2 x ATR14 daily at the entry decision; judged cost and financing as above. They are always or often in the market, so trade counts are small (a few per year per pair); this is declared up front.

**Trials: 11 new (F1 x4, F1_W x2, F2_top3, F3_L120, LT x3) -> N = 156 + 11 = 167.** In-sample Bonferroni alpha = 0.05 / 167 = **2.99e-4**.

## Pass rules (judged: conservative `mt5_measured` cost + measured swaps). The unit is (variant, primary pair); F2 is the pooled book.
**In-sample PASS = all of:**
1. PF >= 1.10.
2. Trades >= the minimum: F1 4h 100; F1 daily 30; F1 daily weekly-filtered 20; F2 30; F3_L120 15; LT1 15; LT2 15; LT3 10.
3. **Drawdown:** |max DD R| <= net R (recovery >= 1) **and** |max DD R| <= 20 R (cap).
4. One-sided t-test mean per-trade R > 0 with p < 2.99e-4.
5. **Folds:** net R > 0 in at least 3 of the 5 calendar folds.

**Holdout PASS (scored once, only for in-sample passes) = all of:** PF > 1.10; net R > 0; |max DD R| <= |in-sample max DD R|; trades >= the holdout minimum (F1 4h 15; F1 daily 5; F1 weekly-filtered 3; F2 5; F3 5; LT 3); one-sided per-trade t-test p < 0.05 / (number of cases sent to holdout).
**Overall PASS** = in-sample PASS AND holdout PASS on EURUSD or on USDJPY (each judged on its own).

**Robustness label (reported, not required):** the 10-pair basket pooled, with in-sample PF >= 1.0 and >= 6/10 pairs with PF > 1.0. It is a backup check; a primary-pair PASS without it is flagged "pair-specific".

**Power (reported honestly):** minimum detectable mean R at 80% power = (z(1 - alpha) + 0.84) x sd(R) / sqrt(n) at alpha 2.99e-4, for every case. **A case with MDE > 0.5 R is labelled UNDERPOWERED, and a FAIL there means "not shown", not "shown zero".**
The LT and weekly-filtered variants are expected to be underpowered. Their trade counts are reported exactly, and nothing is pooled across pairs to hide that.

Failed variants join the banned list and are not re-run under new names. The pre-registration is final: no variant is added after any result is seen.
