The filter cut trades by roughly 15-30% and lowered PF on every primary case and on the pool (0.70). Within this data, trading only with the weekly trend does not help squeeze breakouts. Single pairs underpowered (40-57 trades), pool powered.

After measured costs and swaps no variant passed, the only single-pair PFs above 1.1 (EURUSD LT1 1.34, USDJPY LT2 1.20, EURUSD F3 1.43) are underpowered, fail the p-value and fold criteria, and are not backed by the 10-pair basket, which is clearly negative (PF 0.58-0.65, 1,000+ trades for LT1/LT2).
