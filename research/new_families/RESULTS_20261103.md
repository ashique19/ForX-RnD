# Multi-timeframe confluence round - results (2026-11-03)

Pre-registration: `research/new_families/PREREG_20261103.md` (commit a2ec0d0, made before any variant was run). Harness: `research/htf/confluence_20261103.py` (commit a2bfb2b) with 12 tests, including explicit no-lookahead tests. Research only: no yaml, gate, model or journal change, nothing promoted.

## What was tested
The existing 1h model entries (saved 10-year walk-forward predictions, main-book logic: 1:1 SL/TP at 2 ATR, 8-bar hold, min_conf 0.60) were kept. An entry was taken only if the 4h and/or daily trend agreed with its direction. 4h and daily bars were resampled from the validated 1h history and only fully closed higher-timeframe bars as of the entry decision time were used. Sanity check: the unfiltered baseline reproduced the saved 2026-10-20 trades exactly on all 10 pairs (count and total R identical).

Judged cost = mt5_measured (EURUSD 1.4, USDJPY 2.4 pips round trip) plus the measured MT5 swap snapshot. Reference column = 1.0 pip with no swap. The trial count rose from 167 to 174 (7 variants; alpha = 0.05/174 = 2.87e-4).

## Verdict
**Every judged variant failed in-sample on both EURUSD and USDJPY. The holdout was not run (no case qualified), and none of the 7 variants gets a forward shadow.** Higher-timeframe agreement did not produce more confident or profitable shots.

- On EURUSD the filters cut trades by 47-74% and PF did not improve (0.92-0.96 vs 0.947 baseline). Net R was less negative only because there were fewer trades.
- On USDJPY the filters lifted PF modestly (0.797 to about 0.84-0.85 for H4_EMA50, D_EMA50, H4_AND_D and H4_STRUCT) but stayed well below 1.0 and far below the 1.05 criterion. Folds positive were 0-1 of 5.
- The extra 0.65 confidence cut (H4_D_CONF65) did not help either (EURUSD 0.956; USDJPY 0.765, worse than baseline). The control CONF65 alone was no better than baseline, so there is no "more confident shot" effect in the model's own confidence.
- Counter-check (H4_D_DISAGREE, sanity only, not judged): worse than baseline on USDJPY as expected, but NOT worse on EURUSD (PF 1.032, p = 0.317, so not significant). The bucket diagnostic shows the same thing: on EURUSD the "both oppose" bucket is the best (PF 1.029), and on 10 pairs the order is not monotonic (agree 0.786, mixed 0.728, oppose 0.753). The higher-timeframe trend has no dependable relationship with the 1h entry quality.
- 10-pair backup: the pooled PF edged up from 0.758 to 0.77-0.79 under the filters but stayed far below 1.0. Label: does not support.
- The baseline itself loses money after real costs (EURUSD PF 0.947, USDJPY 0.797 in-sample), so filtering a losing entry set did not rescue it.

## Caveats
- D_EMA50 overlaps the earlier banned daily filters (F1/F2, 2026-10-20) with a different definition. It was a counted re-test and also failed.
- The swap data is a single 2026-10 snapshot applied to history. The effect is small for holds of 8 hours or less. PF without swap is shown alongside.
- The entry model is the 1h walk-forward model. Trading a model that sees higher timeframes as features (rather than a post-filter) was not tested here.

## Banned variants (trial count N=174)
H4_EMA50, D_EMA50, H4_AND_D, H4_D_CONF65, H4_STRUCT, CONF65 (and the H4_D_DISAGREE sanity case) are not to be re-tried with other parameters.
