# Pre-registration: Phase 1 daily lens library on the main-book 1h entries - 2026-11-12

Written and committed BEFORE any Phase 1 result is computed. Paper research only (owner approval t564u, after the 2026-11-12 offer to start Phase 1). Nothing live changes.

This is Phase 1 of the ForX Multi-Lens Learn & Test Plan. The owner's brief: "find a lens which best describes the trend and its always different one." The work is a library of distinct daily-chart trend/regime descriptions for EURUSD and USDJPY, then a pre-registered test of whether agreement between a lens and the existing 1h model's direction improves after-cost outcomes, and a walk-forward (not in-sample) test of picking the "best" lens per regime.

## Hypotheses

- **H1 (agreement).** For at least one pre-registered directional daily lens, keeping only those 1h-model entries whose side agrees with that lens lifts after-cost profit factor above the unfiltered baseline and above the pass bar in section "Pass criteria".
- **H2 (always a different one).** A walk-forward rule that, in each volatility regime, picks the directional lens that was best on past data, and applies only that lens as an agreement filter on the next period, lifts after-cost PF above the unfiltered baseline and above the pass bar. This is the test of "the best lens is always a different one" that does not allow in-sample picking.
- **H0.** Filters only remove trades. After-cost PF stays at or below the baseline, and the baseline stays below 1.0 at the judged costs.

Prior evidence against H1 and H2 (declared, so the bar is not lowered):

- 2026-10-20 (`research/htf/RESULTS_20261020.md`): 10-year baseline loses after costs on all 10 pairs (in-sample PF 0.760, EURUSD 0.95). Daily SMA20/50 (F1) and daily close-vs-SMA20 (F2) did not clear the bar (in-sample PF 0.753 / 0.765; holdout 0.687 / 0.766). Those two filters are banned.
- 2026-10-31 (`research/new_families/RESULTS_20261031.md`): standalone LT1 SMA200, LT2 Donchian 55/20-exit, LT3 MA50/200, and a weekly-trend filter on F1 daily, all failed at the conservative cost. Weekly-trend made F1_D worse (PF 0.43-0.50 vs 0.57-0.71). The 10-pair LT baskets were powered and negative (PF 0.58-0.84).
- 2026-11-03 (`research/new_families/RESULTS_20261103.md`): 4h/daily EMA50 confluence, 4h swing structure, and a confidence control, 0 of 6 judged variants passed. EURUSD PF 0.947 baseline vs 0.92-0.96; USDJPY 0.797 vs 0.77-0.85. The counter-check (both higher timeframes oppose) was not worse on EURUSD (PF 1.032, p=0.32). Banned: H4_EMA50, D_EMA50, H4_AND_D, H4_D_CONF65, H4_STRUCT, CONF65.
- Phase 0 (`research/phase0/RESULTS_20261107.md`): EURUSD after-cost mean R -0.0256, PF 0.940 (3153 trades); the after-cost 95% block CI covers zero. No after-cost edge was shown.

Overlap with those failed families is stated under each lens. A close cousin is a counted re-test, not a free look. Failed Phase 1 variants are banned.

## Entries, data and simulation

- Entries: the saved main-book walk-forward predictions `data/edge_check/htf_20261020/<PAIR>_preds.csv` (champion recipe via `walk_forward_backtest` on validated `data/history/<PAIR>_1h.csv`: xgboost, train 2000 / test 250 / step 250, min_conf 0.60, TP 2 ATR / SL 2 ATR, horizon 8 bars, next-open entry, one position; holdout cutoff 2025-10-01 enforced, no fold trains at or after it). No retrain, no model or yaml change. Columns used: `pred` (post-filter signal), `confidence`.
- The baseline is `pred` unchanged. A variant sets `pred` to HOLD where its condition fails, then re-simulates with the same simulator used on 2026-11-03 (`research/htf/confluence_20261103.py`: one position, next-open entry, TP/SL 2/2 ATR, 8 bars).
- Pairs: EURUSD and USDJPY are each judged on their own. No 10-pair backup is run (Phase 1 is these two pairs only).
- In-sample = entries before 2025-10-01 (first entry 2015-05). Holdout = entries from 2025-10-01 through the last saved prediction (published scores end 2026-10-12 18:00 UTC). The Phase 0 fresh window from 2026-11-01 00:00 UTC onward is not used; the saved predictions do not extend into it.
- Paper-shadow journal (`data/paper_shadow/journal.jsonl`) is a descriptive appendix only: expected counts are single-digit to low-teens closed trades per pair, which is underpowered. It is not a trial, cannot pass, and is not used to pick a lens.

## Daily and weekly bars (resampled from validated 1h history only; nothing is synthesized)

- **Daily bars:** New York 17:00 days exactly as `daily_bars()` in `research/htf/daily_trend_flip_wf_20261020.py` (day ends 17:00 America/New_York; days with fewer than 12 hourly bars dropped). Extended only to carry Open (first), High (max), Low (min), Close (last available 1h close), which the 2026-10-20 helper did not return because F1/F2 used Close alone. Close and day-end are unchanged.
- **Weekly bars:** a week is the Monday-Friday block of those daily bars, labelled by the Monday date. The week's Open/High/Low/Close are the first Open, max High, min Low, and last Close of its daily bars. The week is complete at the `end_utc` of its last daily bar (Friday 17:00 New York when Friday exists and has at least 12 hours). Weeks with fewer than 3 daily bars are dropped.
- **No lookahead.** A 1h signal on bar t is decided when bar t closes, at D = t + 1h, and enters at the next Open. A daily or weekly bar may be used only if its end is at or before D. A bar that is still forming is never used and never partially used. Every lens value used at D is computed from completed bars only. Tests will check: values from full data equal values from data truncated at the decision bar; a violent move inside the forming daily (and weekly) bar changes nothing; the used bar's end is at or before D; a swing pivot is unusable until its confirming bars are complete.

## The lens library (fixed now)

Each directional lens returns +1 (up), -1 (down), or 0 (no call / undefined). A 1h BUY "agrees" only with +1, a 1h SELL only with -1. A value of 0 or NaN never agrees: the entry is skipped. Lenses are computed on completed daily (or weekly) bars and then aligned to each 1h decision with the no-lookahead rule above.

### Directional lenses (each is one agreement-filter trial)

1. **SMA_STACK** (SMA slope/stack). On completed daily closes: SMA20, SMA50, SMA200 (rolling, min_periods equal to the window). Slope of SMA50 = SMA50[t] - SMA50[t-5], needing 5 additional completed days after SMA50 exists. +1 if SMA20 > SMA50 > SMA200 and the SMA50 slope is > 0. -1 if SMA20 < SMA50 < SMA200 and the SMA50 slope is < 0. Otherwise 0 (including any SMA still warming up, or a slope of exactly 0). Overlap: close cousin of banned F1 (SMA20 vs SMA50) and of failed LT3 (MA50/200), counted as a re-test with the stack-and-slope conjunction.

2. **ADX** (ADX trend strength). Wilder's ADX(14) on completed daily bars, with the usual +DM/-DM and +DI/-DI. +1 if ADX >= 25 and +DI > -DI. -1 if ADX >= 25 and -DI > +DI. Otherwise 0 (including ADX warming up, ADX < 25, or +DI == -DI). Threshold 25 is the textbook default, frozen. No prior ForX trial used ADX as a daily filter on these 1h entries.

3. **DONCHIAN** (Donchian position). 20-day Donchian channel on completed daily bars, including the current completed day: High20 = max(High of t-19..t), Low20 = min(Low of t-19..t), 20 bars required. Position = (Close - Low20) / (High20 - Low20); 0 if High20 == Low20. +1 if position >= 2/3. -1 if position <= 1/3. Otherwise 0. Overlap: related to failed LT2 (Donchian 55 with 20-exit) as a standalone system; this is a 20-day position filter on 1h entries, counted as a distinct but related trial. The banned Donchian-55 lookback is not reused.

4. **SWING** (higher-highs / lower-lows swing structure). Swing pivots on completed daily bars with k = 2, same confirmation rule as the 2026-11-03 4h structure lens: bar i is a swing high if its High is strictly above the Highs of the 2 bars before and the 2 bars after, and is confirmed only when bar i+2 has completed; swing lows likewise on Lows. +1 = the last two confirmed swing highs rise AND the last two confirmed swing lows rise (HH and HL). -1 = both fall (LH and LL). Otherwise 0 (or fewer than two confirmed swings of each kind). This is the daily analogue of banned H4_STRUCT, counted as a re-test on a different timeframe, not as a free new idea.

5. **RSI_REGIME** (RSI regime). Wilder's RSI(14) on completed daily closes. +1 if RSI > 50. -1 if RSI < 50. 0 if RSI == 50 or is still warming up. Extremes (RSI >= 70 or RSI <= 30) are reported as a diagnostic bucket on the baseline trades only; they are not a filter and not a trial.

6. **SMA200** (distance from the 200-day average, direction). +1 if Close > SMA200, -1 if Close < SMA200, 0 if equal or SMA200 is warming up (200 completed daily closes). Distance in ATR units, (Close - SMA200) / ATR14 of the daily bars, is a diagnostic bucket only, not a trial. Overlap: close cousin of failed LT1 (SMA200 as a standalone system), counted as a re-test as a filter on 1h entries.

7. **WEEKLY** (weekly-trend agreement). On completed weekly closes, as defined above: sign(weekly Close - SMA10 of weekly Closes), SMA10 with min_periods=10. +1 / -1 / 0 (0 if SMA10 is warming up or the difference is exactly 0). The latest weekly bar used at D is the last week whose end is at or before D. This reuses the 2026-10-31 weekly-trend definition (SMA10 of weekly closes) as a counted re-test, now applied as an agreement filter on the 1h model rather than on F1.

### Non-directional lens, used as the regime axis (not a judged filter)

8. **ATR_VOL** (ATR volatility regime). Daily ATR14 (Wilder's ATR on completed daily bars) versus its trailing 100-day median (rolling median of the last 100 completed ATR14 values, including today, min_periods=100). Regime is **high** if ATR14 > median, **low** otherwise (including ATR14 == median). If the median is undefined, regime is **unknown**. This lens has no direction, so it is not tested as an agreement filter and is not a trial. It is stored in the library and is the only axis used to split regimes for H2.

## Variants and trial count (8 new trials; N goes 174 -> 182; alpha = 0.05/182 = 2.75e-4)

The current published trial count is **174** (167 after the 2026-10-31 round, plus 7 on 2026-11-03). No trial has been added since. Phase 1 adds 8:

- (a)-(g): one agreement filter per directional lens, in the order above: SMA_STACK, ADX, DONCHIAN, SWING, RSI_REGIME, SMA200, WEEKLY. Each is applied to the full in-sample (and, only if it passes, the holdout) independently. EURUSD and USDJPY are two cases of the same variant, not two trials, matching 2026-11-03.
- (h) **BEST_PER_REGIME** (the H2 walk-forward selector). One trial. Protocol, frozen:

  1. Walk-forward years Y = 2016, 2017, ..., 2025. The judged in-sample window for this variant is entries from 2016-01-01 through 2025-09-30 (2015 is used only as the first training block and is not scored for this variant, because no prior year exists). The unfiltered baseline compared against this variant is restricted to the same entry window.
  2. For year Y, training trades are baseline (unfiltered) trades with entry time strictly before Y-01-01, at the judged cost. For each regime {low, high}, among the 7 directional lenses that have at least 30 training trades in that regime (agreement-filtered, so the 30 is the count after the lens filter), pick the lens with the highest training mean after-cost R. Ties break by the fixed order (a) through (g). If that best mean R is not strictly greater than the unfiltered baseline mean R in the same regime and training window, or if no lens has 30 training trades, the selection is **no-filter** (take the 1h signal unchanged) for that regime in year Y. The **unknown** regime is always no-filter.
  3. On year Y's trades (entries in [Y-01-01, Y+1-01-01) and, for 2025, only through 2025-09-30 for the in-sample score), apply the selected lens as an agreement filter, or apply no filter if that was the selection. The combined walk-forward trades are the judged sample.
  4. Holdout (entries from 2025-10-01), scored only if this variant passes the in-sample bar: selections are **frozen** from the Y=2025 step (trained only on entries before 2025-01-01, which is before the holdout; there is no re-selection on 2025 in-sample data and none inside the holdout). Those frozen low/high choices are applied to holdout trades. This is slightly conservative (the last train window ends 2024-12-31) and keeps the holdout clean.

In-sample "which lens had the best PF in regime X" tables are diagnostic only. They cannot pass, cannot change a definition, and are not used to pick a filter after seeing the numbers.

## Costs and financing

- Judged: round-trip research costs as specified, EURUSD **1.4 pips**, USDJPY **2.4 pips**, applied in memory as `spread_pips` (equals `mt5_measured` / Duka p90 on these two pairs). No yaml change.
- Reference, reported but not judged: the same costs plus the measured MT5 swap snapshot (`data/research/swaps/mt5_swaps_20261021_1813Z.json`, same convention as 2026-11-03: interest-on-price per year / 360, Wednesday x3, signed, rollover = 17:00 New York; a trade is charged for each rollover between its entry-bar open and the end of its exit bar). The snapshot is a single 2026-10 reading applied to history. With holds of at most 8 hours its effect is small.
- Per-trade R = net_return x entry / (2 x ATR14 at the decision bar), identical to 2026-11-03.

## Metrics

Reported for the baseline and every variant, per pair, at the judged cost, on the in-sample window (and holdout only if required):

- Trade count, after-cost PF (sum of positive R / |sum of negative R|), expectancy (mean R), net R, max drawdown in cumulative R by entry time, win rate.
- One-sided t-test p-value of mean R > 0.
- 95% time-block bootstrap CI for mean R (block length 20 trades, 1000 draws, seed 7, trades in entry-time order). The CI is reported; the pass test uses the t-test, not "CI excludes zero", so the rule stays consistent with 2026-11-03.
- Trade-count change versus the baseline.
- Five calendar-block folds over [2015-01-01, 2025-10-01); a fold is positive if its net R > 0. For BEST_PER_REGIME the same fold edges are used but only trades in its judged window (from 2016-01-01) count; a fold with zero trades is not positive.

Thin results are called thin: 100 to 299 trades is "thin". Fewer than 100 is UNDERPOWERED and cannot pass.

## Pass criteria (fixed before running; each of EURUSD and USDJPY is a separate case; all eight variants are judged)

**In-sample, all must hold at the judged cost:**

1. Trades at least 100. Fewer than 100 is UNDERPOWERED and cannot pass. 100 to 299 is flagged "thin" but may still pass the other bars.
2. PF at least 1.05 AND at least the same pair's baseline PF + 0.05 (for BEST_PER_REGIME, the baseline is on the 2016-01-01 through 2025-09-30 window).
3. Net R above 0 and |max DD| not larger than net R.
4. One-sided p for mean R above 0 below 2.75e-4 (0.05/182).
5. At least 3 of 5 folds positive.

**Holdout, scored once, only for cases that pass in-sample:** at least 30 trades, PF above 1.0 and at least the baseline holdout PF + 0.05, net R above 0, one-sided p below 0.05 / (number of cases scored).

**Overall PASS = in-sample AND holdout.** There is no re-selection, threshold change, or definition change after any result is seen. Any pass only leads to a written proposal for a frozen forward paper shadow; nothing is enabled live, and nothing is promoted.

## Sanity requirement

Before any variant is read, the baseline re-simulated by the Phase 1 harness at the judged costs must reproduce the saved 2026-10-20 / 2026-11-03 baseline trades on EURUSD and USDJPY (count and total R at p90 / 1.4 and 2.4 pip costs: EURUSD 3153 trades, -80.729 R; USDJPY 2005 trades, -154.674 R, as recorded in the 2026-11-03 sanity). If it does not, stop and report; do not read variant results.
