# Phase 1 results: daily lens library — 2026-11-12

Paper research only. Prereg: `research/phase1/PREREG_20261112.md` (commit 218e3e5), committed and pushed before any result was computed.
Trial count N = 174 + 8 = **182**. Alpha = 0.05/182 = 0.000274725.

## Verdict

**Nothing passed.** 0 of 8 judged variants cleared the in-sample bar on either pair. Holdout was not scored. No forward paper-shadow is proposed. H0 is not rejected. The trial count is now **182**.

All 16 pair-by-variant cases had at least 418 trades (none thin, none underpowered). Every after-cost PF stayed below 1.05, and every net R was negative.

- Closest PF: EURUSD SMA200 at 0.988 versus a 0.948 baseline (plus 0.040, short of the required plus 0.05 and of 1.05), still -7.64 R, 2 of 5 folds positive, p = 0.59.
- USDJPY ADX lifted PF from 0.803 to 0.931 (that does clear plus 0.05 over baseline) but stays below 1.05, with net R -11.36, 2 of 5 folds, p = 0.75.
- BEST_PER_REGIME, the walk-forward test of "the best lens is always a different one", did not beat its matched 2016-01-01 through 2025-09-30 baseline: EURUSD 0.944 versus 0.953 (2602 unfiltered trades in that window); USDJPY 0.851 versus 0.827 (1747 unfiltered; short of plus 0.05). Selections did change across years, but picking last year's winner did not produce an after-cost edge.
- WEEKLY agreement on EURUSD is worse than the baseline, with a 95% block CI entirely below zero (mean R -0.0562, CI [-0.1023, -0.0099]). USDJPY SMA_STACK is likewise entirely below zero.

Diagnostic buckets are not trials and cannot pass. On EURUSD, several oppose buckets are better than agree (SWING oppose PF 1.068, WEEKLY oppose 1.050, ADX oppose 1.005), which matches the 2026-11-03 finding that higher-timeframe agreement has no dependable link to entry quality. EURUSD RSI extremes (n = 94 and 100) are underpowered even as a description.

Failed judged variants, banned: SMA_STACK, ADX, DONCHIAN, SWING, RSI_REGIME, SMA200, WEEKLY, BEST_PER_REGIME.

Harness: 16 tests in `tests/test_phase1_lenses.py` pass, including no-lookahead, weekly completion only at Friday 17:00 New York, and BEST_PER_REGIME selection rules. Sanity reproduced the saved baseline exactly. Code commit bd09a8e was pushed before these results were computed.
